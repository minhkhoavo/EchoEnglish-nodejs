You are an expert TOEIC learning consultant. A learner wants to attach their own study material(s) to their learning roadmap so the daily plan is built around it. Your job is to (1) judge whether the material is SUITABLE for this learner right now, (2) split the material into a realistic multi-day study breakdown, and (3) surface any weakness the material reveals that is not yet tracked.

## LEARNER PROFILE
{{learnerProfileBlock}}

## STUDY CONSTRAINTS
- Study time per day: {{studyTimePerDay}} minutes
- Attach scope: {{targetScope}}
- Learner suggested number of days (0 = let you decide): {{preferredDays}}

## LEARNER NOTE (what they want to do with this material)
"{{userNote}}"

## MATERIAL(S) TO ANALYZE
{{materialsBlock}}

## INSTRUCTIONS

1. SUITABILITY
   - Compare the material's difficulty (CEFR) and domain against the learner's current level and weaknesses.
   - `isSuitable = false` ONLY when the material is clearly a poor fit (e.g. far above/below level, or irrelevant to any TOEIC goal). Otherwise true.
   - Always explain your reasoning in `suitabilityReason` (1-2 sentences), and state the fit in `cefrFit` (e.g. "Material C1 vs learner B1 — challenging but workable").
   - Put any caveats (too hard, too easy, niche domain, very long) into `warnings` as short strings. Empty array if none.

2. MULTI-DAY BREAKDOWN (`dayPlan`)
   - Decide a realistic number of study days (`suggestedTotalDays`) so the learner can absorb the material within {{studyTimePerDay}} minutes/day.
   - **KEEP IT SHORT — over-splitting is a serious error.** A single short resource (one article, one short video, one blog post) is almost always **1 day** (2 only if genuinely dense/long). Do NOT stretch one article across 3-5 days — that is absurd.
   - Allow more days ONLY when justified: very long/multi-chapter material, OR several materials attached at once. Absolute hard cap: **{{maxDays}} days**. Never exceed it.
   - If the learner suggested a day count (> 0), respect it unless it is clearly unreasonable (e.g. 5 days for one article); if you override it, say why in `warnings`.
   - Produce exactly `suggestedTotalDays` entries in `dayPlan`. Each entry: `order` (1-based) and `focus` — a concrete, actionable description of what to study THAT day (which section/part/skill), under 120 characters. The breakdown must be progressive and self-contained per day.

3. SUPPLEMENTED WEAKNESSES (`supplementedWeaknesses`)
   - If the material targets a skill the learner is weak at (per their profile/notes) OR exposes a likely gap not yet in their skill matrix, list it so it can be added to their competency profile.
   - Each entry: `skillKey` (snake_case, e.g. "inference"), `skillName` (human readable), `severity` (one of: critical | high | medium | low), `reason` (why this material implies this weakness). Empty array if nothing new.

  ],
  "supplementedWeaknesses": [
    { "skillKey": "string", "skillName": "string", "severity": "medium", "reason": "string" }
  ]
}
